# Welcome to My Blog

This is the first post on my new blog! I'm excited to share my thoughts and experiences with you.

## About This Blog

This blog supports both English and Sinhala content, making it accessible to a wider audience. The Admin Dashboard allows me to easily create and manage posts without writing any code.

## Features

- **Easy Content Management**: Powered by Decap CMS
- **Multilingual Support**: English and Sinhala
- **Responsive Design**: Works on all devices
- **Automatic Page Generation**: New posts create their own pages automatically

## Getting Started

To access the admin panel, navigate to `/admin/` on your site. From there, you can:

1. Create new blog posts
2. Edit existing posts
3. Upload images
4. Manage content in multiple languages

Thank you for visiting, and I hope you enjoy reading my posts!
